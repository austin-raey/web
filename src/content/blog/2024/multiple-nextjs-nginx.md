## Background

Recently at work, it has come up that we need to incrementally roll out _specific_ parts of our app as we rebuild it with the latest version of NextJS (15). The old app is also based on NextJS (11), but it is an older version and would require non-trivial changes to upgrade &mdash; so we are opting to rebuild the app from scratch.

Prior to this, other upgrades or rewrites nicely had allowed us to directly host the app on a new subdomain and server, because they were small enough to do it all at once. This time, however, we do not have this luxury.
